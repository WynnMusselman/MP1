About Me

Hello! My name is Wynn Musselman, and I am a student at Boston University. I am studying Computer Science with a minor in Political Science. During previous semesters, I interned at BU's EL Connector office and completed an internship in Paris for a tech start-up. Currently, I am the Bytes Lead for the Girls Who Code chapter, a Junior Development Team Lead for the Hack4Impact chapter, a grader for the Computer Science department, and a Community Ambassador for Spark!. For fun, I like to hang out with friends, play video games, code my own projects, and explore Boston!

On this website, you will find my education, experience, project, and other information that may be of interest for you.
