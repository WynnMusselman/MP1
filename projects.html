Society of Math Club Website

August 2026

The Society of Math Club's website was originally created by someone who did not know any web development. Because of this, much of the code was difficult to read and did not follow convention while the design needed improvements. I rewrote almost all the CSS and edited much of the HTML to follow convention. I added many comments to ensure that future developers of the site can make changes easily, even if they don't know how to code. I also fixed the design based on advice from a graphic designer.
